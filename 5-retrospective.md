# Retrospective

Student ID: `c23035992`

Reflecting on my app and its development, I found it hard to get the hang of things at the start due to how different Android development is from anything I’d done before. But as I kept persisting, things started to click. The Android official documentation was a huge help, with great tutorials that helped me understand numerous features within Android.

My initial plan was to have an app that helped users track all things pet-related, and I believe the app I’ve built achieves that. With more time, I would have improved the app further in terms of design, but I also would have liked to implement a community element, such as allowing users to upload and share their favourite walking routes with others on the app.

If I were to do this project again, I’d definitely try experimenting more with different UI elements, as I often stuck to the ones I was already comfortable with. While this meant the app’s functionality was solid, it also led to a lacklustre design. I know Android has endless design possibilities, so I’d want to experiment more with layout variety, animations, and overall styling to give the app a more professional look.
